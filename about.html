ABOUT EUROPEAN GOLDFINCH

A small place on the web.

European Goldfinch is a personal archive for words, memories, notes and messages that are worth preserving.

The design deliberately keeps an old-school web/forum feeling while using a responsive layout that works on modern phones and computers.

Messages published here are part of the site's public archive. Do not place passwords, private addresses or other sensitive information in a public GitHub Pages site.
